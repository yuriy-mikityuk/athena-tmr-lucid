## Takeaway

EazzZyLearn_pc is a real, MIT-licensed, 63-commit Python/PyQt5 closed-loop **NREM slow-oscillation** TMR tool written mainly by David Marcel Baum (PhD neurophysiology, Sorbonne 2018; co-founder/CTO of the Chilean neurotech start-up Helment 2022–2024; since 2025 a Research Engineer at InteraXon, the maker of Muse). It began in Dec 2022 on a Helment EEG board, moved to OpenBCI Cyton (release v2025.06) and gained Muse support in June–July 2025 (v2025.09-BETA) by listening to an **external OSC stream** (`/eeg` plus optional `/muse_metrics`), not by talking BLE to the headset. It stages sleep only as binary *awake?* and *SWS?* — either by Welch band-ratio thresholds whose quoted 85.88 % accuracy was measured on 46 EGI 128-channel lab recordings using Fp1 (not on Muse), or by taking the arg-max of five stage probabilities (Wake/N1/N2/N3/REM) that arrive over OSC from an InteraXon-side classifier that is **not in the repository**. It detects slow-oscillation down-states by zero-crossing plus an adaptive Hilbert-envelope threshold, predicts the up-state with a self-adapting coefficient, plays one `.wav` cue per subject through `sounddevice` with a 6-s refractory period, and writes four comma-separated `.txt` logs. It has no REM gate (the REM probability it receives is never acted on), no motion/IMU input, no volume calibration or cap, no cooldown beyond 6 s, no emergency stop beyond closing the GUI, no seeded cued/uncued assignment, no blind morning retest, no deterministic in-process replay, no tests, no CI, no packaging, no releases after 2025-11-04 (3 stars, 0 forks, 6 issues), and no publication, preprint or preregistration names it. remind-eeg therefore adds a great deal beyond "REM instead of N3", and the report's sentence must change from "no open-source TMR project uses Muse" to a statement that an open NREM-SO TMR tool for Muse exists but no open project other than remind-eeg does REM-gated, safety-guarded, randomised and validator-checked cueing on Muse hardware (corrected wording in Inferences).

### 2. Staging method, update interval, REM

- Binary targets only: `SleepWakeState` keeps `self.isawake` and `self.issws`; `staging()` is called with `'isawake'` (every `WAKE_STAGE_INTERVAL = 1` s on a `REPONSE_BUFFER_LENGTH = 3` s window) or `'issws'` (every `SLEEP_STAGE_INTERVAL = 5` s on a `SLEEP_BUFFER_LENGTH = 30` s window) — [sleep_wake_state.py l.6-80](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/backend/sleep_wake_state.py), [parameters.py l.101-117, l.138-140](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/parameters.py)
- Traditional mode: Welch PSD (`win = 4 * sample_rate`), band-ratio thresholds "SWS if >": DeltaVSBeta 295, DeltaVSGamma 1500, SpindleVSBeta 3, SpindleVSGamma 15, ThetaVSBeta 20, ThetaVSGamma 100; "Awake if": SpindleVSTheta 0.3, BetaVSSpindle 0.5, BetaVSTheta 0.09, Gamma 0.5, GammaVSDelta 0.001, GammaVSAlpha 0.075, GammaVSSpindle 0.2, GammaVSTheta 0.5; bands Delta 0.5-4, Theta 4-8, Alpha 8-12, Spindle 12-16, Beta 12-40, Gamma 25-45 Hz; causal Butterworth order 3 with 50/60 Hz notch — [parameters.py l.165-213](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/parameters.py), [sleep_wake_state.py l.186-250](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/backend/sleep_wake_state.py)
- The accuracy figure's provenance is a code comment: "Evaluated on n=46 EGI HGSC128 datasets using Fp1: SWS staging accuracy separated at NREM1: 85.88 +/- 10.57 %; Wake classification separated at NREM1: 88.64 +/- 10.17 %" — i.e. a binary SWS-vs-rest / wake-vs-rest accuracy on 128-channel laboratory EGI recordings using the Fp1 electrode, **not** a Muse validation and not a 5-stage or REM metric; the README's "85.88% accuracy" and CLAUDE.md's "Research-validated thresholds" rest on this unpublished comment — [parameters.py l.182-185](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/parameters.py), [README l.9](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/README.md), [CLAUDE.md l.84](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/CLAUDE.md)
- Movement guard inside staging: `LOCKING_LENGTH = 120` s — "If during the LOCKING_LENGTH time, the majority of scores were Awake, then, no matter the wake staging, awake will be scored as True. This is used in order to prevent large movements to be interpretated as delta activity"; implemented as a rolling boolean buffer with a ≥50 % rule — [parameters.py l.141-147](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/parameters.py), [sleep_wake_state.py l.122-131](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/backend/sleep_wake_state.py)

### 3. Cue timing, stimulus and library

- Down-state detection: last positive-to-negative zero crossing in a 5-s delta (0.5-4 Hz) buffer (`DELTA_BUFFER_LENGTH = 256*5`); validation requires ≥2 samples after the trough (upward trend), trough below an adaptive threshold computed as −(mean + `SD_MULTIPLICATOR` 1.1 × SD) of the Hilbert envelope over a 5-s `THRESHOLD_BUFFER_LENGTH`, updated at most every 500 ms (`DEFAULT_THRESHOLD = -75 µV` start), and above an artifact threshold (`NON_PHYSIOLOGICAL_THRESHOLD = -300 µV`, later adapted to 2.5× the mean of the last three validated down-states); comments cite Göldi et al. 2019 (refractory 8 s) and Ngo et al. 2013 (threshold window 5 s) — [predict_slow_oscillation.py l.8-183](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/backend/predict_slow_oscillation.py), [parameters.py l.104-132](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/parameters.py)
- Up-state prediction: trough-to-stimulus delay = measured down-time × `TROUGH_MULTIPLICATION = 1.25`, adapted online ("retrospective learning", rolling buffer of 100 coefficients, mean of last 3) — [README l.297-311](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/README.md), [predict_slow_oscillation.py l.27-30](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/backend/predict_slow_oscillation.py)
- Trigger rule: stimulate when `stim_at_stamp` is reached, not awake, in SWS, and outside the refractory window; `LEN_REFRACTORY = 6  # seconds; Arbitrary, but "enough" (~Göldi)`; README: refractory "indication from Schreiner et al., 2015, Nature Communications that cue+feedback destroys CMR effects". No maximum number of cues per night, no minimum inter-cue interval beyond 6 s, no per-REM/NREM-block budget — [backend.py l.171-197](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/backend/backend.py), [parameters.py l.76-77](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/parameters.py), [README l.91](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/README.md)
- Stimulus: one `.wav` per subject (`SUBJECT_INFO['chosencue'] = 'gong'`) plus a looping background sound (`'Meditative_Mind_P1F9MiPr2Vs_short'`) with a linear fade; `Sounds/` holds `gong.wav`, `heart.wav`, `Meditative_Mind_P1F9MiPr2Vs_short.wav`, `SOUNDS.md` (new cues must be added by hand to the settings-dialog combo `["gong", "heart", …]`). Playback is `sounddevice.sd.play(cue_array, sample_rate)` with `sd.default.latency = 'low'`; `cueing.py` contains no volume scaling or cap (the only amplitude operation is the fade of the background sound) — [parameters.py l.24-31, l.78](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/parameters.py), [Sounds/](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/tree/main/Sounds), [SOUNDS.md](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/Sounds/SOUNDS.md), [cueing.py l.1-73](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/backend/cueing.py)
- Encoding phase (`main_STUDY.py`): "background sounds with periodic cue sounds during cognitive tasks", period `SUBJECT_INFO['cueinterval']` in minutes (default 1) — [README l.34-40](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/README.md), [cueing.py l.21](https://github.com/SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc/blob/main/src/backend/cueing.py)
- remind-eeg: `TmrSchedulerConfig(puzzle_cue_interval_seconds=30.0, cooldown_seconds=120.0)`; the scheduler "logs scheduling decisions only. Audio playback remains behind `AudioCuePlayer`, and REM confidence remains behind `StableRemGate`"; skip reason codes `scheduler_cooldown_active`, `cue_interval_active`, `tlr_interval_active`; cue library metadata is separate from private audio (`cue_library.py`) and TLR blocks are planned by `plan_tlr_block` with a `post_block_pause_seconds` — [tmr_scheduler.py l.64-66, l.124-129, l.198, l.214, l.359](https://github.com/yuriy-mikityuk/remind-eeg/blob/main/src/muse_tmr/protocol/tmr_scheduler.py), [tlr_protocol.py l.201, l.384](https://github.com/yuriy-mikityuk/remind-eeg/blob/main/src/muse_tmr/protocol/tlr_protocol.py)

### 4. Safety

## Inferences

- The categorical claim is wrong as written and should be replaced. Corrected sentence for the report: **"An open-source closed-loop TMR system that runs on Muse does exist — SoftcodingForYou/EazzZyLearn_pc (MIT, Dec 2022–Nov 2025, 63 commits, by a neurophysiologist now at InteraXon) cues at predicted slow-oscillation up-states during N3 on Muse 2/Muse S/Athena (via an external OSC stream) and OpenBCI Cyton — but no open project other than remind-eeg performs REM-gated cueing on Muse hardware: EazzZyLearn receives a REM probability and never acts on it, and it has no arousal guard, volume calibration or cap, seeded cued/uncued assignment, blind retest, deterministic replay, automated tests or staged pilot validators."** The same correction applies to `sleep_staging_tools.md` l.85/l.110 ("no open staging code … no GitHub project") — open Muse staging code exists (band-ratio and OSC-metrics stagers in EazzZyLearn, YASA-based `southsko/muse-sleep-dashboard`, Omega741's threshold classes), none PSG-validated.
