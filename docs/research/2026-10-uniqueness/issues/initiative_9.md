# Initiative 9: Versioned, labelled synthetic night corpus in the Athena TAG format and a hardware-free demo command

## Why

Every "synthetic recording" in the test suite is empty. Four test files (the notes count three) carry their own copy of the helper: `write_synthetic_recording()` in `tests/test_replay.py` line 19, `tests/test_pilot3_replay_simulation.py` line 134 and `tests/test_rem_annotations.py` line 45, and `write_replay_recording()` in `tests/test_epochs.py` line 71. Each writes three zero-filled packets (`bytes(28)`, `bytes(36)`, `bytes(60)`; the optics table entry at `muse_athena_protocol.py` line 59 is 40 bytes). Zero counts decode to −725 µV DC on all four channels (`eeg_counts_to_uv_centered`, lines 222–226): format-valid, physiologically empty, which is why the saturated eye proxy of #116 passed every test. REM-like signal exists only as in-memory sines (`rem_like_eeg_channels()`, `tests/test_pilot3_replay_simulation.py` lines 24–35), never as TAG packets; the only real bytes are the 28,122-byte awake capture `tests/test_data/muse_20250824_172756.bin`. The README quick start (`README.md` lines 35–73) needs a headband at its second command, and no job in `.github/workflows/guardrails.yml` runs replay → detector → gate → scheduler → report.

No public Muse overnight dataset exists on Zenodo, PhysioNet or OpenNeuro as of 2026-10-21 (docs/research/2026-10-uniqueness/notes/verify_wearable_rem_physiology_open_science.md row 18). Realism comes from the repository's own measurements: #108 (23.34 %/h drain on `p1034`, 10.01 %/h on `p21`, death at 2 h 29 min; the completed night: 835,926 notifications over 7.998 h), #160 (43 repeated subpackets in 23 min, 1.0 % lost EEG and 0.4 % lost optics subpackets, ≈257.2 Hz effective rate), #154 (63.9 Hz line up to ≈40 µV on AF7), and from the ZMax F7/F8-to-Fpz montage, where REM counts against PSG EOG gave r = 0.66 with ≈1.8× over-counting attributed to the near-Fpz reference (Esfahani et al. 2023, bioRxiv, https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.08.18.553744v1). Synthetic REM morphology is an assumption, not a measurement.

Of the inspected projects none ships a labelled synthetic overnight corpus in device format: southsko/muse-sleep-dashboard, the closest, has a `smoke_test.py` with four unlabelled synthetic CSVs that "prove code paths only, not real sleep quality" (notes/muse_ecosystem.md); Taran-Douley/closed-loop-sleep simulates on Sleep-EDF PSG (notes/verify_cueing_tools_landscape.md row 9); EazzZyLearn_pc re-streams a user's own data over OSC (notes/gap_1_direct_open_source_competitor_softcoding.md). Honesty is 3/5 in the judgement: synthetic agreement is test infrastructure, never evidence, every artefact must say "synthetic input, not sleep", and nothing here claims REM accuracy or efficacy.

- [ ] `uncued_puzzle_play_count == 0` on every scenario and `clean_night` produces ≥ 1 cued `play` event (so the end-to-end check is not satisfied by an all-paused night; this needs the guard fix 10a); `demo` asserts `audio_backend == "mock"`; the diff touches nothing under `src/muse_tmr/models/`, `protocol/`, `audio/`, so `P_REM` reaches audio only through `StableRemGate` and `ArousalGuard`; no weight or threshold fitted to synthetic data.
