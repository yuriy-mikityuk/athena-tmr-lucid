# Initiative 6: Standard data exchange: EDF+/BIDS export with cue annotations, MNE Raw, and import of hypnograms and external recordings

## Why

Nothing in `src/muse_tmr` reads or writes EDF, BDF, BIDS or MNE: `*.bdf` and `*.edf` are forbidden (`scripts/check_forbidden_files.py` lines 15, 22) and gitignored (`.gitignore` lines 41–42), and `pyproject.toml` (lines 48–77) has no EDF or MNE dependency. The only continuous-EEG reconstruction is `_collect()` in `scripts/validate_rem_detection.py` (lines 79–150): zero-fill of frame gaps above 0.5 s at 256 Hz (lines 72–73) and `sfreq = n_samples / (t_last − t0)` (line 147). Its docstring (line 12) names `decoded_frames.jsonl` but line 129 replays `raw_amused.bin`, and its comment at line 160 says "~254.8 Hz" where #160 measured ≈257.2 Hz; the code wins; measure the rate per night. `ReplaySession` resolves only `raw_amused.bin` (`src/muse_tmr/data/replay.py` line 129), so the recorder's `decoded_frames.jsonl` (`src/muse_tmr/data/recorder.py` line 346) has no replay path (#108) although `MuseFrame.from_json` exists (`sample_types.py` line 287). OpenMuse and BrainFlow values enter `EEGSample` with no declared scale (`openmuse_lsl_source.py` line 365, `brainflow_source.py` line 463).

Every open stager takes EDF/EDF+ or an MNE `Raw` and resamples internally: YASA to 100 Hz, U-Sleep to 128 Hz (Perslev et al. 2021, npj Digit Med, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8050216/), ezscore-f to 64 Hz. EDF+ defines TAL annotations and the standard hypnogram texts (https://www.edfplus.info/specs/edfplus.html). BIDS has no sleep extension (closest BEP044/BEP045, https://bids.neuroimaging.io/extensions/beps.html); validator-passing practice is a second `acq-scoring` events file (Mikkelsen et al. 2025, Sci Data, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04579-8; https://neurostars.org/t/how-to-add-sleep-scorings/21965). BOAS (OpenNeuro ds005555, CC0) and Wearanize+ (Sikder et al. 2025, Sleep Advances, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12888818/) ship as BIDS/EDF. Decoder scales diverge: OpenMuse leaves a ≈725 µV pedestal, BrainFlow before 5.23.0 was ≈4.55× too large and uncentred (https://github.com/brainflow-dev/brainflow/pull/855, /pull/856), Mind Monitor units are unverified, and the 1450 µV full scale in `muse_athena_protocol.py` lines 75–76 (paths of top-level BLE modules are given at HEAD; after Initiative 8 PR B they live under `src/muse_tmr/ble/`) is vendor-stated with no primary document (docs/research/2026-10-uniqueness/notes/gap_4_the_ocular_channel_on_muse_why_the_eye_m.md KQ A).

No inspected project fills this. neurofeed (https://github.com/windwerfer/neurofeed) writes EDF/CSV with no sleep context and no Athena optics; eeg-neurolyze (https://github.com/MatPomGit/eeg-neurolyze) has EEG-BIDS export "not yet tested with an actual Muse S Athena"; EazzZyLearn_pc writes comma-separated `.txt`; Dreamento relies on Hypnodyne's recorder; InteraXon sells EDF/CSV export to research partners only (https://musehealth.ai/). None exports a Muse night with cue, gate and guard events as standard annotations or lets a night recorded by another app run through a REM-gating simulation.

## Scope

- [ ] `src/muse_tmr/data/continuous.py`: `ContinuousConfig(gap_policy="zero_fill"|"discontinuous", gap_threshold_seconds=0.5, nominal_rate_hz=256.0)`, `reconstruct_continuous(frames) -> ContinuousRecording` (float32 arrays per channel, `t0`, `measured_rate_hz`, `segments`, `gap_markers` from frame gaps and `EEGSample.gaps`, PR #182). `_collect()` in `scripts/validate_rem_detection.py` calls it; fix lines 12 and 160.
- [ ] `src/muse_tmr/data/night_events.py`: `collect_night_annotations(recording_dir)` → `(onset_s, duration_s, text)` from `events.jsonl` (incl. `emergency_stop_triggered`), `scheduler_events.jsonl` (`play/skip/pause/stop` with reason codes; gate transitions from `rem_gate_open`/`rem_gate_closed`), `arousal_guard_events.jsonl`, `audio_playback.jsonl`, `awakening_events.jsonl`, and hypnogram files. Lossy map, recorded: `wake→Sleep stage W`, `probable_rem→Sleep stage R`, `unknown→Sleep stage ?`, `nrem→Sleep stage N` (or a documented non-standard text); never N1/N2/N3.
- [ ] `src/muse_tmr/data/edf_io.py`: `write_edf_plus()`, `read_edf() -> Iterator[MuseFrame]`, `read_edf_hypnogram()` via one optional library (`edfio`, else `pyedflib`) in a new `[edf]` extra. EEG physical range ±725 µV so 14-bit counts stay lossless in 16-bit; nominal 256 Hz as record rate, measured rate and gap policy in the header; IMU in `g`/`deg/s`, optics as raw `MISC`; EDF+C (annotated gaps) default, EDF+D optional; TAL track from `night_events`.
- [ ] `src/muse_tmr/data/mne_export.py`: `to_raw(...) -> mne.io.RawArray` (µV→V, eeg/misc types, `Annotations` from the same collector), `mne` imported lazily.
- [ ] `src/muse_tmr/data/bids_export.py`: `sub-<id>/ses-<date>/eeg/` with `_task-sleep_eeg.edf`, `_channels.tsv` (EEG/ACCEL/GYRO/MISC, units), `_eeg.json` (`EEGReference: "Fpz (vendor-stated, unverified)"` plus the required fields), `_events.tsv` for cue/gate/guard events, `_acq-scoring_events.tsv` for stages, `dataset_description.json`, `participants.tsv` with a pseudonymous id; HED-SCORE off by default.
- [ ] `src/muse_tmr/data/import_recording.py`: `import_recording(path, fmt, out_dir, eeg_scale, eeg_offset)` for `decoded-frames`, `edf`, `muselsl-csv`, `openmuse-csv`, `brainflow-csv`, `mindmonitor-csv`, writing `decoded_frames.jsonl`, `metadata.json` (`source.source_name="import:<fmt>"`, `capabilities.raw_packets=false`, `import` block with scale, offset, provenance, clock) and `summary.json` with `raw_packet_count=0`. `IMPORT_SCALE_TABLE` with provenance per row (OpenMuse −725 µV; BrainFlow <5.23.0 ÷4.55 and centre; Mind Monitor `unverified`, so `--eeg-scale` is mandatory). `resolve_raw_path` (`replay.py` lines 126–134) falls back to `decoded_frames.jsonl` and streams `MuseFrame.from_json` rows. `annotate-template`, `simulate-replay-cues` and the HTML report refuse imports without a declared scale.
- [ ] `src/muse_tmr/cli/main.py`: `export-edf`, `export-bids`, `import`, `annotate-template --from-hypnogram <edf|events.tsv|csv>` (W→`wake`, N/N1/N2/N3→`nrem`, R→`probable_rem`, else `unknown`; `prediction_source="hypnogram:<file>"`). `README.md` lines 33 and 147: 41 → 44 (or the current count plus three, if earlier initiatives have already moved it).
- [ ] Docs: new `docs/formats.md` (layouts, range, gap policies, lossy map, Luna's epoch-0-at-EDF-start assumption, https://zzz.bwh.harvard.edu/luna-walkthrough/p3/hypno) and `docs/import.md` (per-format loss matrix, scale table, "report and simulation only": Pilot 1 requires `raw_packet_count > 0`, `pilot1.py` line 168); update `docs/data_model.md` lines 78–91, `docs/USAGE.md`, README, `CHANGELOG.md`.
- [ ] Tests: `tests/test_continuous.py`, `test_edf_io.py`, `test_bids_export.py`, `test_import_recording.py` (CSV fixtures generated in-test; `*.csv` is gitignored, `.gitignore` line 38), `test_mne_export.py` (skipped without `mne`), parse tests in `tests/test_cli.py`; new modules in `run_tests.py`.
- [ ] `.github/workflows/guardrails.yml`: `tests` installs `.[dev,edf]`; new `bids` job exports a synthetic night and runs `bids-validator` under the Node 22 setup the `page` job uses.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `python run_tests.py --all` passes; `policy`, `tests`, `package`, `page` and the new `bids` job are green; a base `pip install .` still imports `muse_tmr.data` and runs `replay`.
- [ ] The PR description reports: round-trip sample counts, max |Δ| in µV (≤ 1 count = 0.0885 µV), max annotation-onset error (≤ 1 ms), measured rate, EDF size of an 8 h synthetic night, `bids-validator` error and warning counts.
- [ ] `scripts/validate_rem_detection.py` yields identical `sfreq`, `n_samples` and channel arrays before and after the refactor; a stride-30 replay of a `raw_amused.bin` night gives byte-identical scheduler events.
- [ ] `replay`, the nightly report (`muse_tmr.reports.nightly_report.build_report` after Initiative 9, else `scripts/generate_nightly_report.py` line 45), `annotate-template` and `simulate-replay-cues` accept an imported directory unchanged; a test asserts `validate-pilot1-recording` fails it on `raw_packet_count > 0`; `--from-hypnogram` rows load through `load_rem_annotations` and `train-rem-classifier`.
- [ ] No module under `muse_tmr.data` imports `muse_tmr.audio`, `muse_tmr.protocol` or `muse_tmr.models`; scheduler, gate, guard and player modules byte-identical to `main`; no exporter or importer emits `play`; uncued puzzles stay unschedulable; P_REM reaches audio only through the gate.
- [ ] `eeg.json` and `docs/formats.md` state the 1450 µV full scale is vendor-stated and unverified; imported-night reports carry an "imported: <format>, scale <x>; report and simulation only" banner; no text claims REM accuracy or efficacy.
- [ ] `python scripts/check_forbidden_files.py` and `git diff --check` clean; no `.edf`, `.csv`, recording, hypnogram or device identifier committed; `ALLOWLIST` unchanged unless the maintainer approves one fixture.
