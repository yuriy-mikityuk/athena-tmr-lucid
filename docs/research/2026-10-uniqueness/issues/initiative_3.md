# Initiative 3: Cue-locked arousal scoring with paired quiet control windows as the primary safety metric

## Why

The repository decides *before* a cue and never measures what happens *after* it. `ArousalGuard.evaluate()` (`src/muse_tmr/protocol/arousal_guard.py` lines 160–167) is prospective, works on 30 s feature rows and uses thresholds no file justifies (lines 26–39). In the Pilot 4 loop (`src/muse_tmr/validation/pilot4.py` lines 343–372) a `play` reaches `_play_scheduler_event` (line 466) and nothing looks at the next 10 s. The only cue-window code, `CueMovementLog` (`src/muse_tmr/features/imu_features.py` lines 79–100, −2/+10 s), needs `cue_timestamps` (line 192) that no caller passes (`pilot3.py` line 226, `pilot4.py` line 349), so the "cue-window motion count" of `docs/validation.md` (line 47) and the guard's `cue_related_arousal` rule (`arousal_guard.py` lines 295–296) are dead in every pilot. `Pilot4CueingSummary` (lines 110–147) has no per-cue field and `AwakeningEvent` (lines 226–243) has only a wall-clock `timestamp_utc`. No audio night has been run (report Part 1); the first one is uninterpretable without this scorer.

Post-cue arousal is the field's safety metric. AASM arousals within 10 s followed 57.9 % of loud (≈43 dBA) and 43.0 % of soft (≈28 dBA) cues, and cued-with-arousal items were forgotten more than uncued ones (t(22) = 3.28, p = .003): "the degree of arousal is more important than the objective intensity of the cue" (Whitmore & Paller 2023, Learn Mem, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10027237). Home TLR cues gave 0.19 movement events per cue versus 0.10 in silent periods, p = .002, the paired-quiet-window design copied here (Konkoly et al. 2024, Conscious Cogn, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11542932). Multisensory REM cues disrupted 23.7 % of cued REM periods (Picard-Deland et al. 2024, bioRxiv preprint, v2 Aug 2026, https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.06.21.600133v2.full); Coon et al. 2026, Front Neurosci (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12914948/) ask for logged stimuli and arousals and stricter settings in REM (report §3, notes/strategy_science.md §4).

Of the projects inspected, none computes arousal per cue from its own logs with silent controls. The closest, home-tmr (https://github.com/nathanww/home-tmr), backs off after an arousal, but from Fitbit motion/HR, for N3, with per-cue statistics only in the SleepStim paper; dormant since 2022-12. COsleep disengages on arousal but publishes no per-cue statistics; SMACC's log is experimenter-driven; EazzZyLearn_pc has no arousal input (notes/cueing_tools_landscape.md). Forehead EEG lacks occipital alpha and chin EMG, which "limits arousal detection and Wake vs N1 distinction" (same preprint, v1): every flag below is a proxy, volume is a software gain, and nothing here measures benefit.

## Scope

- [ ] `src/muse_tmr/reports/cue_arousal.py`: `CueArousalConfig` (post window 10 s, baseline 20 s, control exclusion ±60 s; alpha 8–12, beta 15–30, EMG 55–95 Hz with the 64 Hz line bridged via `complexity_features.bridge_lines`; IMU thresholds from `IMUFeatureConfig`; HR jump 10 bpm; rise thresholds marked UNFITTED), per-window score records, `CueArousalReport` (`schema_version`, `limitations`, `to_markdown`/`save`/`load` as in `reports/analysis.py`) and `score_cue_arousal()`.
- [ ] Window cutter in that module: one `ReplaySession` pass over `raw_amused.bin` cutting EEG/IMU/PPG/HR samples at absolute times for every cue and control, flagging BLE gaps instead of treating zero-fill as signal (model: `scripts/validate_rem_detection.py` `_collect`, lines 79–130).
- [ ] Time base and gate log: `metadata.json` (`pilot4.py` lines 690–715) omits the first-epoch start behind scheduler `timestamp_seconds` (line 345) and the gate decision is dropped once consumed (lines 351–359); so `first_epoch_start` goes into `pilot4_summary.json` and Pilot 3 `metrics` (fallback: replay's first frame plus limitation `time_base_inferred`) and `pilot4.py` writes `rem_gate_decisions.jsonl` per epoch (Pilot 3 already keeps `epochs[]`). A control is a virtual cue time in the same gate state with no `play` within ±60 s, scored with the same windows; a "REM period" is a contiguous gate-open run.
- [ ] `AwakeningEvent` gains optional `timestamp_seconds` and `attributed_to_cue` (`yes|no|unsure`), `log-pilot4-awakening` (`cli/main.py` lines 438–445) the matching flags; old rows load.
- [ ] Outcomes: `arousal_proxy_per_cue` and `arousal_proxy_per_control` by flag (`alpha`, `beta`, `emg`, `imu`, `hr`, `any`) with their difference; `cue_attributed_awakening_rate` (≤120 s after a `play`); `rem_periods_with_disruption_fraction`; `rem_termination_within_60s_of_cue_rate`; dose tables by effective-volume bin (`audio_playback.jsonl`), inter-cue interval and `puzzle_cue_index`/`tlr_event_index`; `limitations` such as `ppg_absent`, `thresholds_unfitted`, `synthetic_input`.
- [ ] Outputs and CLI (`cli/main.py`): `cue_arousal_events.jsonl`, `cue_arousal_report.json`/`.md`; `muse-tmr score-cue-arousal <recording_dir>` with flags for the inputs above; `run-pilot4-cueing`/`run-pilot5-full-night` run the scorer on their own recording at the end and embed a `cue_arousal` block in the summary; `simulate-replay-cues --inject-cues-every-seconds N` emits synthetic `play` events in gate-open epochs (mock audio, tagged `synthetic_cue`) to check window placement on a replayed night first.
- [ ] Docs: new `docs/safety_outcomes.md` (definitions; the 43–58 %, 0.19 vs 0.10 and 23.7 % benchmarks beside the repo's proxies; limitations); `docs/validation.md` "Safety Outcome Metrics" table after line 53; `docs/pilot4_low_volume_cueing.md`; `docs/pilot5_full_night.md`; `docs/pilot3_replay_cue_simulation.md`; `docs/USAGE.md`; `docs/data_model.md`; `docs/safety.md`; README (42 commands); `CHANGELOG.md`.
- [ ] Tests: `tests/test_cue_arousal.py` (an alpha burst 2–8 s after a cue flags `alpha` and its control does not; a 55–95 Hz burst flags `emg` but a 40 µV 64 Hz line alone does not; missing PPG gives `ppg_absent`, not a flag; controls never within ±60 s of a `play` and in the cue's gate state; on the Initiative 9 `clean_night` scenario, cues injected at `arousals.csv` times flag far more often than cues at quiet times); updates to `tests/test_pilot4_cueing.py`, `tests/test_pilot3_replay_simulation.py`, `tests/test_cli.py`; `tests.test_cue_arousal` in `run_tests.py`.

## Out of scope

- Two-way volume titration and a persisted set-point (deferred in notes/strategy_judgement.md until `arousal_per_cue` exists on real nights); strategy_technical.md's "validators fail above a ceiling", for the same reason.
- Passing cue timestamps into `extract_imu_features()` in the live Pilot 3/4 loops: it would wake the dormant `cue_related_arousal` pause rule and change sleep-time behaviour; the scorer reuses the −2/+10 s window, and the live hook is a follow-up once a baseline exists.
- Sham/no-cue arms (Initiative 4); protocol presets (Initiative 7); EDF export (Initiative 6); the synthetic generator itself (Initiative 9); detector or gate changes (Initiatives 1, 2); tonic/phasic sub-gate; any live BLE or real-audio run; any validation of the proxies against PSG-scored arousals.
