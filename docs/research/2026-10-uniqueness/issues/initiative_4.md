# Initiative 4: Sham and no-cue nights, N-of-1 blocks, delayed retest, blind incorporation coding and SCRIBE/CENT export

## Why

Item-level control is the engine's most rigorous part: `PuzzleCueAssignment.ensure_schedulable()` (`src/muse_tmr/protocol/randomization.py` lines 83–88) makes uncued puzzles unschedulable, `_scheduled_puzzles()` builds cues only from `scheduled_puzzle_ids` (`src/muse_tmr/protocol/tmr_scheduler.py` lines 478–490), and the Morning tab never learns `cue_condition` (`src/muse_tmr/app/morning.py` line 8 and lines 204–222; `tests/test_app_morning.py` line 90). Night-level control does not exist. At HEAD `083649b` a grep of `src/` and `docs/` for `sham`, `no_cue`, `nof1`, `retest_wave`, `rater` finds nothing (the only `arm` hits are the contact gate's verb `arm()`); `TmrSchedulerEvent` has no arm field (`tmr_scheduler.py` lines 80–87); `MorningRetest` knows one wave (`src/muse_tmr/reports/morning_retest.py` line 85); `DreamReport` holds `lucid`, `cues_heard`, `confidence`, `dream_text` and self-linked incorporations only (`src/muse_tmr/reports/dream_report.py` lines 17–23, 57–68); `_limitations()` in `src/muse_tmr/reports/analysis.py` (lines 469–558) has seven codes, none about arms, blinding or delayed testing; `docs/pilot4_low_volume_cueing.md` line 124 admits "an empty cue night can still pass". Every cued-vs-uncued delta is confounded with "a night on which sounds played".

The field moved its controls to the night level. The one randomised, participant-blind home TLR study interleaved untrained-sound and no-cue nights with TLR nights (interaction F(1,118) = 6.28, p = .01; no-cue vs TLR p = .77; Konkoly et al. 2024, Consciousness and Cognition, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11542932). The REM puzzle-cueing study lists "no no-cue night" and incomplete blinding as limitations; blind-coded conceptual incorporation (β = 1.24, p = .037), not cueing (30 % vs 22 %, p > .6), predicted solving (Konkoly et al. 2026, Neuroscience of Consciousness, https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2026/1/niaf067/8456489). REM-TMR effects appeared only at one week (d = 0.646, day-1 p = .55; Pereira et al. 2023, J Neurosci, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10218979/) or ten days (Rakowska et al. 2021, NeuroImage, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8591408/); home TMR helped only on night 3 (Göldi & Rasch 2019, npj Sci Learn, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6497651/). A sham-controlled multi-centre preprint found induction no different from sham although cues were perceived in 71.1 % of cued REM periods (Picard-Deland et al. 2024, bioRxiv v2 2026, https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.06.21.600133v2.full). Single-case reporting has standards: SCRIBE 2016 (Tate et al. 2016, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4960517/) and N-of-1 analysis with carry-over and autocorrelation (Schmid & Yang 2022, HDSR, https://hdsr.mitpress.mit.edu/pub/b6efwlql; Konigorski et al. 2026, Exp Physiol, https://doi.org/10.1113/EP092753); no N-of-1 TMR/TLR study exists in the notes (docs/research/2026-10-uniqueness/notes/tmr_tlr_science.md KQ7).

Of the repositories inspected, none seals night arms over physiology-gated cues: COsleep only obfuscates sham/stim and is stale since 2023, Taran-Douley lists sham nights as unimplemented, brainwave_opensync has seeded blinded n-of-1 blocks without physiology (notes/cueing_tools_landscape.md KQ4; notes/gap_1_direct_open_source_competitor_softcoding.md §9). Nothing here measures efficacy: at n = 1 the experimenter cannot be blind, a sham still makes sound, the untrained sound may be recognised, and every output is a single-case description.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `python run_tests.py --all` and the `policy`, `tests`, `package`, `page` jobs pass; `node scripts/check_page.mjs` passes with the new Morning fields.
- [ ] A replayed synthetic night under `no_cue` yields zero scheduler `play` events and zero `status == "played"` rows in `audio_playback.jsonl`, with `virtual_play` skips where the `cue` arm plays; the same seed under `cue` and `sham` gives identical timestamps and volumes, differing only in `cue_id` and `arm`.

- [ ] Schema-1 dream reports, retests and assignments load unchanged; `run-pilot5-full-night` without `--arm` behaves exactly as today.
- [ ] The PR description reports, labelled "synthetic input, not sleep": the permutation interval on a 12-night synthetic series with and without a planted arm difference, κ for a two-rater synthetic packet, and the count of SCRIBE items filled `auto` vs `manual`.

## Out of scope

- Automatic LRLR detection (rejected: no algorithm with a reported sensitivity exists, notes/gap_4 KQ B); the ±60 s AF7−AF8 plot and EDF+ annotation export of eye-signal attempts (follow-up after Initiatives 1 and 6).
- Computing `arousal_proxy_per_cue` (Initiative 3); the preset registry, manifest hash and sound→puzzle manipulation check (Initiative 7); README status and citation wording (Initiative 5); the synthetic night corpus (Initiative 9).
- Two-way volume titration, tonic/phasic sub-gate, Bayesian models with external priors, aggregation across participants, power heuristics (none exist), any real night.
