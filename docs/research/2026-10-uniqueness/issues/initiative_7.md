# Initiative 7: Replace the dead configs/*.yaml with a loadable, citable protocol-preset registry, a fidelity report and a manipulation check

## Why

The four `configs/*.yaml` files are loaded by nothing (`grep -rn yaml src scripts` is empty) and contradict the code: `rem_detector.yaml` says 0.7 and a 30 s stable window against 0.80/0.70 and 60 s in `src/muse_tmr/models/rem_gate.py` lines 45–49; `audio.yaml` says 1.5 s fades and a 60 s cooldown against 0.25 s in `src/muse_tmr/audio/audio_player.py` lines 19–22 and 120 s in `src/muse_tmr/protocol/tmr_scheduler.py` lines 63–68. `docs/protocol.md` line 18 still points at `protocol_konkoly_like.yaml`; "Konkoly-like" (`README.md` lines 3, 115) is the only attribution and nothing cites Konkoly 2021, Carr 2020 or Sanders 2019 (notes/verify_repo_inventory.md A7, A12). The real protocol is a dozen CLI flags (`src/muse_tmr/cli/main.py` lines 1975–2017, 2061–2134) that `_write_metadata` (`src/muse_tmr/validation/pilot4.py` lines 690–714) and `Pilot4CueingSummary` (lines 110–147) never record; `docs/validation.md` line 53 checks TLR fidelity only against the local block plan; the 396 Hz / 1 s / 3-repetition TLR default (`src/muse_tmr/protocol/tlr_protocol.py` lines 24–29, 43–47, 197–201) is unexplained.

Verified parameter sources: Konkoly et al. 2021, Curr Biol (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2021.01.026), three arms; Konkoly et al. 2024, Conscious Cogn (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11542932), two experiments; Konkoly et al. 2026, Neurosci Conscious (https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2026/1/niaf067/8456489); Sanders et al. 2019, Psychol Sci (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6843748), recall check 71.9 %, no cued/uncued difference, 2020 correction unchecked; Borghese et al. 2022, Front Psychiatry (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9281560/; notes only); Picard-Deland et al. 2024, bioRxiv preprint (https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.06.21.600133v1.full); Schwartz et al. 2022, Curr Biol (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2022.09.032). Their platforms, Dreem and ZMax, are discontinued or closed (Salfi et al. 2025, J Sleep Res, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12592824/).

Of the repositories inspected, none encodes published cueing parameters as versioned, cited, machine-checked presets. SMACC (https://github.com/remrama/smacc), the closest, is a manual Windows experimenter console without presets or a fidelity check; Dreamento leaves its stimulation-to-stage coupling undocumented; rmallela26/TLR and matthewrennie/tlr are timers. No efficacy is claimed: the deliverable is provenance and a fidelity measurement.

- [ ] A pilot test asserts that `metadata.json`, the summary, the Pilot 3 report and the analysis JSON carry one `protocol.content_hash`; the PR description lists every preset's hash and `source_check` plus the synthetic fidelity table, labelled "synthetic input, not sleep".
- [ ] No preset builder sets `max_volume`, `default_volume` or `volume_hint`; `IntensityRamp` and dB SPL fields are documentation and fidelity-report inputs only; a test asserts that every runnable preset's builders leave `hard_max_volume` 0.20 and the calibrated comfortable volume untouched (AGENTS.md audio rule).
